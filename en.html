FAQ

Good to know

What happens at a first session?

We spend a few minutes talking about your needs and tension. Please fill in the health questionnaire before you come, so you can make the most of your treatment time.

Are sessions reimbursed?

Not at the moment. I am training towards ASCA / RME accreditation; until then, sessions are paid by the client.

How do I cancel or move an appointment?

At least 24 hours in advance, by email or WhatsApp. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged in full.

I have a health condition. Can I come?

Mention it in the health questionnaire or message me before the session. The massage is adapted, or postponed if there is a contraindication. It does not replace medical advice.

Where can I park?

The easiest option is the Avanchets shopping centre car park, 2 minutes’ walk away. There are also blue zone spaces (parking disc) on chemin de Joinville and the public car park of the Crowne Plaza hotel.
